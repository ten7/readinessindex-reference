The seven dimensions

By Ivan Stegic, 2026-09-25

Version 11.3 of the Readiness Index spreads a hundred points across seven dimensions and fifty-one tests. Each page below says what one dimension measures and what this site does about it — including where it falls short.

The dimensions

Primary Indicators
30 points across 13 tests. The thirteen highest-signal tests in the Readiness Index, all answerable from a single page.
Rendering and extraction
15 points across 6 tests. Whether the facts survive without JavaScript, in a structure a parser can follow.
Structured data and the entity graph
15 points across 8 tests. Whether the markup forms one coherent graph, and whether every node in it is valid.
Crawl, index and licensing hygiene
12 points across 10 tests. What the organization has said a crawler may keep, and whether crawl budget reaches real pages.
Authorship, provenance, freshness
12 points across 6 tests. Trust signals expressed as structured data rather than as vibes.
Agent interfaces
8 points across 4 tests. The move from a site an agent can read to a site an agent can use.
Alternate representations
8 points across 4 tests. Clean, low-noise versions of the content served alongside the HTML.

# Contact

By Ivan Stegic, 20 September 2026

There is no contact form on this site, and that is a deliberate choice rather than an
omission. A static site cannot process a form without handing the work to somebody
else's server, and the services that do it tend to arrive with a bot challenge attached
— which is the precise thing two of the tests in this Index exist to catch. A labelled
email address is also the thing an agent acting on your behalf can actually use. So:
write to us, and a person will answer.
